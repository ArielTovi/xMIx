Task 1:

Initially i started the model and asked it to Say hello. This was its answer: "Hello! It's an honor to meet you and wish you a pleasant journey of knowledge and understanding."
After writing an xMIx application the adds a constant vector (of 8's) to the residual stream in each layer, its output was complete gibberish and not even in english (or ascii).
It is worth noting that the SteeringVectorScaledAdder (which i used in the application) uses triton, so i had to modify its 'forward'
function to manually add the vectors in case triton isn't available.

To make sure the gibberish was a valid result and not a broken model I tried a few things:
- Apply a 2nd application which steers with -8. This didn't work because it conflicts with the previous one.
- Remove the addition and create a new application that writes twice in each layer. +8 and -8.
  This didn't work because it conflicts with itself (which took me a few minutes to realize xD).
My takeaway was that I can't steer at the same stage at the same layer from multiple apps (all tries were to all layers at mlp.post).

My final tests were to change the constant vector.
We know 8 was total gibberish.
0.1 was in ascii and had words, but they were random and didn't mean anything.
0.001 resulted in a coherent 'Hello' message with slightly different wording from the first.
This was enough for me to conclude that the application successfully changed the vectors at each layer.
